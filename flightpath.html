Flightpath

A service for the PE-backed CEO

Being a CEO is a privilege and can be the best job in the world. The job is also hard and lonely. You must project confidence and certainty to your team and regulate your emotional energy even when results are going against you.

You report to your board and your chair reports to your investors. No conversation with a colleague, a board member or an investor is free of risk. Your C-suite can turn to you; you have nobody to turn to.

Flightpath exists to solve that problem for CEOs.

The service

A trusted confidant who has sat where you sit.

You can say anything to me. I have done your job, I will not judge your state of mind and I am on the end of the phone when you need to talk.

My client list stays very short so that I can be present for every CEO I work with.

Included as standard
  • Fixed weekly slot for a one-hour video call or meeting
  • Availability for the rest of the week. I return any missed call within 24 hours
  • Board pack reviews. Clients mostly ask for these on specific contentious matters
  • Optional preparation before each founder or investor meeting
  • Quarterly Flightpath review and rolling three-month planner
  • Second opinion on senior hires and exits
Flightpath is not
  • CoachingCoaches are valuable. I am not one; I give you my opinion whenever you ask.
  • A NEDA NED owes fiduciary duties to the company. My client is you and my job is to help you perform at your best.
  • Consultancy projectsConsultants deliver recommendations and special projects for companies. I sometimes do that work; in Flightpath the project is you.
My commitment to you

My client is you, the CEO. You and you alone can fire me.

You engage me; the board and the founders do not. Your company pays my fee and my only interest is your performance. The objective is your success, happiness and fulfilment.

I do not meet investors or boards, work with other executives in your company or give third-party interviews about the companies and funds my CEOs work with.

My commitments
  • Empathy
  • Radical honesty
  • Timeliness
  • Full commitment
  • Discretion
  • No ego
Your commitments
  • Show up physically and mentally
  • Stay present
  • Be fully honest
Discretion

Our work depends on discretion. Nothing you tell me reaches your investors, your chair, other board members, your team or another client. I publish no client list and no testimonials. My invoice reads “Flightpath”.

You can fire me and I can end the engagement if basic standards are breached. Flightpath works only where trust runs both ways and both of us understand those standards.

Case for Flightpath

It is both the best job in the world and the loneliest.

A CEO's job is hard. You bear the weight of the business every day and are expected to manage the pressure without showing strain. Some expectations of you are never spelled out, especially soon after a move to private equity ownership. Board meetings and reporting come round relentlessly while your energy and pace must never drop.

You look out for your team and their welfare. Nobody inside the company does the same for you or offers a conversation free of risk.

An outlet, a non-judgemental confidant and an empathetic sounding board can be essential to a CEO performing at their best.
