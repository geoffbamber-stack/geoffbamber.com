Flightpath

A service for the PE-backed CEO

Being a CEO is a true privilege, it can be the best job in the world. But it is not an easy job and it can be lonely. There are no risk-free conversations with your colleagues, Board members or investors. You need to project confidence and certainty to your team and regulate your emotional energy even when things aren't going right.

You report to your Board and the Chair reports to your investors so no conversation feels risk free.

Your C-suite can turn to you but where can you turn?

Flightpath is designed to solve this problem for CEOs.

What is Flightpath

A trusted confidant who has sat where you sit.

Flightpath gives you a trusted confidant who has sat where you sit and who you can say whatever you need to. I will not judge you for how you are feeling and will answer the phone whenever you need to speak.

I work with a very small group of clients at any one time in order to ensure that I can be present for them when they need me.

As standard, Flightpath includes
  • A fixed slot for a one-hour video call (or meeting) every week
  • Availability when you need during the rest of the week. If I am not immediately available, I will return your call within 24 hours
  • Board pack reviews. My CEOs tend to require this for specific, potentially controversial matters
  • Optional preparation before each founder or investor meeting
  • A quarterly Flightpath review and rolling 3 month planner
  • Second opinion on senior hires and exits
What it is not
  • CoachingCoaches are great and really valuable. I am not that – if you want my opinion, I will give it.
  • A NEDA NED has fiduciary duties and obligations to the Company. My client is you and I am there to help you perform at your best.
  • Consultancy projectsConsultants exist to deliver recommendations and special projects for companies. I sometimes do that work, but in Flightpath, you are the project.
My commitment to you

My client is you, the CEO. You and you alone can fire me.

I am engaged by you, not the Board, not the founders. My fee is paid by your Company but my specific interest is ensuring that you perform. My objective is your success, happiness and fulfilment.

I do not meet investors or Boards.
I do not work with other Executives within your Company.
I do not do third party interviews about companies or funds that my CEOs work with.

What you can expect from me
  • Empathy
  • Radical honesty
  • Timeliness
  • Full commitment
  • Discretion
  • No ego
What I expect from my clients
  • You show up, physically and mentally
  • You are present
  • You are fully honest
Discretion

A quick word on discretion. Discretion is essential for our work together to be fruitful. Nothing you tell me reaches your investors, your chair or other Board members, your team or another client. I do not publish a client list or testimonials. My invoice reads “Flightpath”.

Just as you have the right to fire me, I also retain the right to terminate the client relationship if basic standards are breached. Trust is essential for Flightpath to be effective and mutual understanding of core standards is a must.

Why does Flightpath work

It is both the best job in the world and the loneliest.

A CEO's job is hard. It is both the best job in the world and the loneliest.

You are carrying the load of your business every day and you are expected to manage the pressure without showing strain. There are often expectations of you that no-one has laid out, especially when you have recently transitioned to Private Equity ownership. The cadence of Board meetings and burden of reporting can be relentless and yet your energy and pace needs to never wane.

As the CEO, you're looking out for your team and their welfare. Who is looking out for you and giving you guidance? Who can you be truly open with in a risk-free manner?

Having an outlet, a non-judgemental confidant and an empathetic sounding board can be essential for CEOs to perform at their best.
